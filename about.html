About

One CAD class started all of this

Going into high school, my older brother told me to sign up for a Computer Aided Design class. I did — and it stuck. One class turned into three, and by the end my teacher pointed me at something bigger: keep going, at the college level, while still in high school. So I did that too.

Two years of college CAD later, I'd figured out the thing that now drives everything on this site: I love designing parts. Not just drawing them — deciding what they should be. Why this shape, why this material, why it'll survive what it has to survive. That's what pointed me at mechanical engineering, and it's the kind of engineer I'm working toward: one who designs parts that don't exist yet.

Along the way I picked up a 3D printer, and CAD stopped being homework. When a design can become a real object the same afternoon, you iterate differently — you find out fast whether the wall's too thin or the fit's too tight. That loop eventually turned into my first real product, which now lives in a hospital. That story is in the projects.
