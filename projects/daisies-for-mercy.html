Case study

Daisies for Mercy

A custom 3D-printed pin that Mercy Hospital uses to honor its nurses. I designed, printed, and delivered every one of them myself.

StatusIn production
SinceFeb 2025
CustomerMercy Hospital
Units served2

Overview

Mercy Hospital takes part in the DAISY Award, a program where nurses are recognized by their coworkers for the care they give. They wanted a small physical pin to go with it.

I design and 3D print the daisy pins for them. Each pin starts as a CAD model, gets printed in matte PLA on my Bambu Lab printer, and I deliver them to the hospital in person. Nurses put a coworker's name on a daisy to honor them.

The daisy itself is a simple part, but I handle the whole process, including the design, printing, quoting, delivery, and payment. Right now two units at the hospital, Postpartum and the NICU, use the daisies.

Process

  1. Model the daisy in CAD. I went through several versions of the petal shape so it would look right and still print cleanly face down without supports.
  2. Slice and tune. I set up the print in Bambu Studio for matte PLA and adjusted the settings until a full batch printed reliably.
  3. Print in batches. I print enough at a time to keep each unit stocked without wasting material on failed prints.
  4. Deliver and manage. I quote each order, print it, bring it to the hospital, and handle payment, then do it again whenever a unit reorders.

What I learned

This project taught me things a school assignment couldn't, like designing for the people who will actually use the part, keeping production consistent from batch to batch, and being responsible for getting every order right.
